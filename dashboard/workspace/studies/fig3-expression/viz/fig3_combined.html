Fidelity: HONEST FIDELITY NOTE: Karr 2012 Fig 3 concerns chromosome-wide DNA–protein interactions (occupancy, % chromosome explored, replication–transcription collisions) from 128 full-chromosome simulations. This reduced viva-Mgen model does not simulate chromosome or DNA-binding dynamics. Panels A–D are clearly-labeled reduced stand-ins built from what we do model — stochastic gene expression on a 48-gene representative panel (single cell, seed 0, 1 s resolution, 60 min). Panels E/F (collision matrix / collisions-vs-density) have no analog and are shown as an explicit “not modeled” placeholder — no chromosome or collision data is fabricated. Model t50 = 2.4 min is the reduced-panel value, not the paper's ~18 min over ~500 genes.
